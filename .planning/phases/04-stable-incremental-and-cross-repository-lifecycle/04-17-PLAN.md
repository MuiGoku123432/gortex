## Gap closed

VERIFICATION `gaps[0]` (status failed): "SC-5 / SEC-01 / D-13 / D-20: every source surface enforces the same repository scope" (04-12 truths 1 and 2; 04-10 prohibition "MUST NOT let two surfaces disagree"). This plan covers the second option of `missing` item 1 (requirePathSourceScope at the remaining choke points) and all of `missing` item 2 (the extended test). Plan 04-18 covers the first option of item 1 (the outer walk stops at another tracked root) and `missing` item 3 (no alpha/nested nodes in the nested fixture).

Choice (orchestrator asked to decide and justify): **both, and they are independent.**
- The gate fix is required on its own. A walk fix cannot remove duplicate nodes that an outer repository indexed before the nested repository was tracked, and those nodes persist until the outer repository's next reindex. The duplicates also exist in every store built before the fix.
- Routing every surface through the existing `requirePathSourceScope` means one function decides ownership, so the surfaces cannot drift apart again.
- No new helper is introduced. The search_text and find_declaration filters each repeat a two-line "anchor the path, then gate it" step with a `// note:` naming the other copy. That is two uses, below the rule of three.
