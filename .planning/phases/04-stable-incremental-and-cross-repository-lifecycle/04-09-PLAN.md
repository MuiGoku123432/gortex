<objective>
Invalidate COBOL observations whenever what they were derived from changes, even with unchanged bytes (ID-08). Derive a COBOL material fingerprint from the extractor's own stamped identity plus the `index.cobol` config it actually reads (D-06, D-07), persist it in `RepoIndexState`, and prove the config-change sequence of D-04 against clean builds. Plan 04-16 (wave 8) then salts the fingerprint into Merkle leaves, proves the extractor-identity change, and re-stamps the VCS commit when HEAD moves without content change (D-17).

Purpose: today `prov_extractor_version` is never compared on reindex and the COBOL extractor reads no config; without this plan a config or version change leaves stale bindings and provenance in place.
Output: `MaterialIdentity`, `internal/indexer/cobol_material.go`, staleness wiring, and the config-change harness tests.
</objective>
