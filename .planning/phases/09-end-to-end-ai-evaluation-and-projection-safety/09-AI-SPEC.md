# AI-SPEC -- Phase 9: End-to-End Evaluation and Storage Projection Decision

> AI design contract generated by `/gsd-ai-integration-phase`. Consumed by `gsd-planner` and `gsd-eval-auditor`.
> This is a planning contract. It does not authorize implementation, provider use, Neo4j synchronization, or automatic claim promotion.

---

## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Hybrid evaluation system for structured extraction and evidence-linked code analysis

**Description:**
Phase 9 evaluates the bounded AI claim workflow delivered by Phases 6 and 7 against representative COBOL and mainframe evidence. The system under evaluation uses Gortex's existing Go LLM provider abstraction to propose closed-schema, separately stored claims about deterministic findings. The Phase 9 harness measures validity, grounding, review outcomes, security, deterministic isolation, and operational behavior. Good means every accepted claim is schema-valid and supported by in-scope evidence, unsafe behavior is blocked before persistence or disclosure, human outcomes are measurable, and enabling AI does not change the canonical deterministic graph.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. A malformed, out-of-scope, stale, or unsupported claim is persisted or exposed beyond experimental use.
2. Proprietary source or canary material reaches an unauthorized provider, log, claim, temporary file, trace, or Cypher export.
3. AI-on and AI-off runs produce different canonical active deterministic subgraphs for identical inputs.
4. Evaluation averages hide a catastrophic case, an undefined denominator, or a materially underrepresented COBOL construct or gap family.
5. Neo4j availability or synchronization becomes an implicit release dependency without passing the measured storage decision gate.

## 1b. Domain Context

**Industry Vertical:** Mainframe application modernization and developer tooling

**User Population:** Senior COBOL/mainframe engineers, modernization architects, graph/data engineers, security reviewers, and release owners evaluating an incomplete estate under repository, workspace, and project scope.

**Stakes Level:** High

**Output Consequence:** Accepted claims may guide impact analysis, parser/extractor investment, dependency investigation, and modernization planning. A wrong or leaked claim can misdirect high-cost migration work, conceal an unresolved boundary, cross tenant/repository scope, or disclose proprietary source. AI claims never become deterministic graph truth.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

| Dimension | Good: expert accepts | Bad: expert flags | Stakes | Source |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------------|--------|--------|
| COBOL semantic fit | Assertion matches the cited program-owned paragraph, data item, copybook, SQL, CICS, or IDMS evidence and preserves dialect/context limits. | Treats a same-named artifact as identical, invents copybook contents, or overstates behavior beyond static evidence. | High | `REQUIREMENTS.md` DET-01..12, GAP-01..08 |
| Evidence sufficiency | Every material assertion is supported by active, in-scope graph IDs or exact ranges; missing context is declared. | Citation exists syntactically but does not entail the assertion, is stale, is out of scope, or omits a decisive gap. | Critical | CLAIM-04, CLAIM-06, REVIEW-05 |
| Epistemic separation | Output distinguishes deterministic facts, unresolved evidence, AI inference, and human review state. | Confidence or reviewer action rewrites parser truth, closes a deterministic gap, or fabricates unavailable source. | Critical | Product Principle, CLAIM-02, CLAIM-08..09 |
| Scope correctness | Repository/workspace/project and source revision match the authorized evaluation case. | Evidence or context crosses scope, binds a duplicate name from another repository, or loses revision lineage. | Critical | SEC-01..03, SEC-06 |
| Actionability | Claim is concise, specific, reviewable, and identifies what evidence or retrieval would confirm or refute it. | Vague modernization advice, unsupported certainty, or no path for reviewer disposition. | Medium | REVIEW-01..06, EVAL-08 |
| Lifecycle correctness | Review and staleness outcomes preserve original model/evidence lineage and coexist with conflicts. | Last-write-wins mutation, duplicated active retry, or trusted visibility after evidence invalidation. | High | CLAIM-07, REVIEW-03..06 |

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

1. Duplicate paragraph, program, copybook, table, map, or transaction names cause plausible but cross-scope citations.
2. A parse-recovery tail is interpreted as absent source, or a missing artifact is described as if its contents were retrieved.
3. Static call/control-flow evidence is overstated as runtime behavior or complete estate coverage despite unresolved lower bounds.
4. SQL aliases and CTEs are reported as physical DB2 tables; dynamic calls are reported as resolved literals.
5. Parser, source, context-builder, prompt, schema, or model changes leave an apparently current claim backed by stale evidence.
6. Reviewers accept a plausible explanation while overlooking a citation that is real but does not support the asserted relationship.
7. Aggregate quality improves while a critical security, scope, or deterministic-isolation canary regresses.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

No single industry regulation is assumed by this milestone. The binding constraints are proprietary-source confidentiality, organizational provider/model/region/retention authorization, least-content processing, owner-only confidential audit artifacts, bounded retention, and auditable reviewer identity. Provider technical support is not authorization. Any estate subject to additional financial, privacy, export-control, or records-retention rules must supply a stricter policy before evaluation or dispatch.

### Domain Expert Roles for Evaluation

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Senior COBOL/mainframe engineer | Label semantic correctness, evidence sufficiency, unresolved boundaries, and claim disposition. |
| Modernization architect | Validate query usefulness, impact interpretation, and whether claims support real decisions without overstating completeness. |
| Security/privacy reviewer | Own provider authorization assumptions, canary design, sink scans, retention, and release-blocking disclosure decisions. |
| Graph/storage engineer | Verify canonical snapshot equality, citation identity/range resolution, lifecycle behavior, native benchmarks, and Cypher fidelity. |
| Independent adjudicator | Resolve label disagreements and calibrate any future LLM judge against human outcomes. |

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** Native Go evaluation harness using existing Gortex provider and graph abstractions

**Version:** Go module/toolchain pinned by the implementation phase; evaluation contract version `phase8-eval-v1`

**Rationale:**
Phase 9 is not building an agent, RAG application, or provider layer. It evaluates an existing structured claim pipeline whose security and determinism depend on Gortex's native scope, SQLite, provider, schema, and review contracts. A direct Go harness can execute the production path, compare canonical SQLite snapshots, validate citations against the exact graph generation, scan controlled sinks, and emit auditable machine-readable results without introducing a second orchestration model. Deterministic code metrics are authoritative; human review supplies domain judgment. An LLM judge is optional research only after calibration and is never a release oracle.

**Model Provider:** No provider is selected by this specification. Evaluation matrices use only deployments explicitly authorized by Phase 7 policy. AI-off is a mandatory baseline.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| Promptfoo | Useful for prompt regression, but cannot by itself prove SQLite graph equality, graph-ID/range validity, claim lifecycle, or all local disclosure sinks. It may be reconsidered as a thin optional runner only if it adds value without becoming authoritative. |
| Arize Phoenix | Strong trace visualization, but raw traces are an additional disclosure and retention boundary. Phase 9 requires metadata-only native records; Phoenix is not the default until security policy explicitly approves it. |
| RAGAS | The system is not retrieval Q&A, and generic faithfulness metrics do not validate Gortex graph identities, source ranges, or deterministic isolation. |
| LangGraph/LangChain/LlamaIndex | No orchestration or retrieval framework is needed. Adding one violates the milestone constraint against a new LLM/orchestration framework and would test a path different from production. |
| Neo4j | Not an AI framework or evaluation implementation. It remains a measured storage projection decision gate after native query and export evidence is collected. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** No. The harness targets the existing model-agnostic Gortex provider interface and records provider/model lineage per run.

### Common Pitfalls

1. Measuring response-level JSON validity while ignoring semantically invalid individual claims or citations.
2. Letting non-deterministic provider output change fixture setup, expected deterministic snapshots, or storage decision measurements.
3. Logging raw prompts/responses through a generic tracing tool to make evaluation convenient.
4. Reporting only means; critical cases, construct families, and security canaries require zero-tolerance gates.
5. Treating a Neo4j benchmark win as authorization for synchronization instead of input to a separate ADR and phase.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:**
Use the exact provider/model/deployment authorized by Phase 7 policy. Record provider, model, endpoint class/region, prompt/context/schema versions, and decoding settings. Prefer the provider's most deterministic supported settings, but never claim repeatability from temperature alone. Run at least three repetitions per AI-enabled reference case for variance characterization. AI-off requires no provider construction or process/network invocation.

**Core Pattern:**
Build one table-driven, production-path evaluation harness in Go. Load immutable cases, execute deterministic setup, capture a canonical baseline, invoke only the real policy/context/provider/validator/claim/review boundary, score deterministic checks before human labels, scan every controlled sink, compare AI-on/off snapshots, and write a versioned metadata-only report. Do not create an evaluation-only claim ingestion path.

**Tool Use:**
Use existing native CLI/MCP/query APIs where acceptance requires user-visible behavior, SQLite snapshots for authority comparisons, the existing provider abstraction for authorized calls, and the Cypher exporter only for scoped snapshot fidelity tests. Evaluation tools are read-only except for isolated test databases and claim/review state created by the test case. No arbitrary model-selected tools or shell execution.

**State Management:**
Each run uses an isolated temporary SQLite authority and explicitly bounded filesystem roots. Persist only normalized scores, IDs/digests, versions, timings, decisions, and sink-scan outcomes by default. Human labels are immutable/versioned; adjudication creates a new label version. Claims and reviews retain normal production lineage. Never use Neo4j as evaluation state or authority.

**Context Window Strategy:**
Use the Phase 7 deterministic context manifest and fixed policy budgets. Cases assert expected evidence IDs, omissions, unavailable artifacts, redaction classes/counts, and digest. Reject over-budget contexts rather than truncating silently. Keep source records clearly delimited as untrusted data and include prompt-injection canaries in comments, literals, paths, findings, and prior claims.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

### Structured Outputs with Pydantic

Production validation remains the closed versioned schema implemented in Go. This Pydantic model is normative evaluator notation for dataset validation and optional offline analysis only; it does not add Python to the product. Export JSON Schema from the canonical Phase 8 schema or verify structural equivalence in CI rather than maintaining two independent contracts.

```python
from pydantic import BaseModel, ConfigDict, Field, model_validator

class EvidenceRef(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra="forbid")
    graph_id: str | None = None
    source_path: str | None = None
    start_row: int | None = Field(default=None, ge=0)
    start_column: int | None = Field(default=None, ge=0)
    end_row: int | None = Field(default=None, ge=0)
    end_column: int | None = Field(default=None, ge=0)

    @model_validator(mode="after")
    def exactly_one_reference_form(self):
        by_id = self.graph_id is not None
        by_range = self.source_path is not None and self.start_row is not None and self.end_row is not None
        if by_id == by_range:
            raise ValueError("cite one graph ID or one exact source range")
        return self

class EvaluatedClaim(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra="forbid")
    schema_version: str
    assertion: str = Field(min_length=1, max_length=2000)
    confidence: float = Field(ge=0.0, le=1.0)
    rationale: str = Field(min_length=1, max_length=4000)
    missing_context: list[str]
    evidence: list[EvidenceRef] = Field(min_length=1, max_length=20)
```

Malformed outputs receive at most the production pipeline's bounded structured-output retry. The original failed payload is not logged by default. Exhaustion records metadata-only failure and creates no claim.

### Async-First Design

Provider evaluations may run concurrently only across isolated cases with fixed concurrency and rate limits. Preserve deterministic case IDs and stable report ordering. Await all sink capture and audit flushes before scanning or deleting a case root. Do not stream partial model output into persistence; validate the complete envelope first.

### Prompt Engineering Discipline

Version system instructions, context serialization, examples, and output schema independently. Keep policy and scope enforcement outside prompts. Label repository content as untrusted evidence, require explicit missing-context declarations, and include few-shot examples for supported, contradicted, and abstaining outcomes. Any prompt change creates a new experiment cohort rather than overwriting prior results.

### Context Window Management

Select only the finding range, containing hierarchy, relevant declarations/dataflow, approved dependencies, and explicit omissions. Record IDs and digest rather than raw context. Dataset coverage must include both sufficient-context cases and cases where the correct behavior is abstention or a missing-context declaration.

### Cost and Latency Budget

No fixed currency or latency target is invented before provider and deployment approval. Every run reports request count, input/output tokens where available, wall time, retry count, and estimated cost keyed by provider/model. CI uses AI-off and recorded/schema-negative checks by default; paid/live provider regression runs require explicit authorization and execute on a controlled release cadence.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

### Measurement Rules

- Score every material claim independently, then aggregate by case, construct family, provider/model, and corpus version.
- `schema validity = schema-valid response attempts / all completed response attempts`; policy denials and provider transport failures are reported separately and never removed from operational denominators.
- `citation validity = semantically valid citation references / all citation references`; a reference is valid only if it exists in the authorized active snapshot, matches scope/revision, and supports the specific assertion.
- `unsupported claim rate = unsupported material claims / all material claims`; malformed responses with no parseable claims are schema failures, not silently counted as zero claims.
- Review outcome distribution reports counts and percentages for confirm, contradict, reject, supersede, and pending, plus inter-reviewer agreement and adjudication rate.
- Report 95% Wilson confidence intervals for rates. Thresholds apply to aggregate results and each critical construct/gap family; no family can be hidden by overall volume.
- Security, scope, mutation, and deterministic-equality failures are atomic release blockers regardless of aggregate score.

### Dimensions

| Dimension | Rubric | Measurement Approach | Release Threshold | Priority |
|-----------|--------|----------------------|-------------------|----------|
| Closed-schema validity | Pass only when strict schema/version validation accepts all types, enums, bounds, cardinalities, and unknown-field rules before persistence. | Code | 100% of persisted claims; >= 99% of completed attempts across the reference set | Critical |
| Evidence reference integrity | Pass each citation only when ID/range exists, is active, in scope and revision, and resolves to the cited bytes/entity. | Code | 100% for persisted claims | Critical |
| Evidence entailment | 5 = all material assertions directly supported; 3 = useful but qualified/partially supported; 1 = citation does not support assertion or decisive context is omitted. | Dual human review; optional calibrated LLM judge for triage only | >= 95% claims score 4-5; zero score-1 persisted/exposed claims | Critical |
| Unsupported claim rate | Unsupported means no cited evidence entails the material assertion, including invented missing artifacts or runtime behavior inferred from static evidence. | Human + deterministic citation checks | <= 2% experimental outputs; 0% among claims eligible beyond experimental use | Critical |
| Missing-context/abstention quality | Pass when incomplete evidence is declared and the output avoids unsupported completion. | Human | >= 95% correct behavior on designated incomplete cases | High |
| COBOL/mainframe semantic correctness | Correct construct ownership and distinctions for structure, data, copybooks, calls, IDMS, CICS, SQL, parse gaps, and missing artifacts. | Human + code labels | >= 95% pass overall and >= 90% in every named family; zero fabricated artifact contents | Critical |
| Human review outcomes | Distribution is visible and disagreements retain both labels until adjudicated; confirmation alone is not quality proof. | Human workflow metrics | 100% labeled release sample; Cohen's kappa >= 0.70 or adjudicate all disagreements and revise rubric | High |
| AI-on/off deterministic equality | Canonical active deterministic node, edge, finding IDs and provenance are byte-for-byte equal after normalization. | Code | Exact equality for every case and lifecycle sequence | Critical |
| Security canary containment | Synthetic markers absent from every unauthorized provider capture and forbidden sink. | Code + security review | Zero leaks, zero unauthorized outbound bytes | Critical |
| Lifecycle correctness | Retries deduplicate; evidence/version changes stale or supersede claims; conflicts coexist; deterministic findings are not closed by AI. | Code | 100% expected transitions | Critical |
| Recurring gap prioritization | Ranking reflects frequency and affected programs/inbound graph impact without treating AI confidence as evidence. | Code + architect review | Exact fixture ordering and documented tie-breaks | Medium |

### Eval Tooling

**Primary Tool:** Native Go table-driven evaluator plus standard `go test`; metadata-only OpenTelemetry-compatible records if an existing approved telemetry sink is available

**Tracing Override:** Arize Phoenix is intentionally not the default. The security contract prohibits raw prompt/source/response logging by default, and no external tracing retention policy is approved. Reconsider only after explicit sink authorization and canary validation.

**Setup:**
```bash

```

**CI/CD Integration:**
```bash

# Live provider matrix is a separate explicitly authorized release job, never an implicit PR cost.
```

### Reference Dataset

**Size:** Start with 24-36 high-quality cases and at least 60 independently labeled material claims. Freeze the release corpus before threshold measurement; do not tune prompts against hidden holdout labels.

**Composition:**
- Representative COBOL programs and copybooks with DATA DIVISION items, duplicate/program-owned names, and exact ranges.
- Literal and dynamic calls, `PERFORM`/`THRU`/`GO TO`, and lower-bound path cases.
- IDMS record/set, CICS program/transaction/map/mapset, and DB2 read/write evidence including aliases, CTEs, and dynamic SQL negatives.
- `PARSE_UNRESOLVED` tails/`ERROR`/`MISSING` and distinct `EXTERNAL_UNRESOLVED` missing-artifact cases.
- Sufficient evidence, deliberately insufficient evidence, conflicting evidence, stale evidence, unknown IDs, out-of-scope duplicate IDs/names, and prompt-injection content.
- Rerun, edit, delete, restore, parser-version change, interrupted retry, provider failure, policy denial, and validation failure sequences.
- Synthetic security canaries in source, comments, literals, paths, neighboring repositories, prompts, prior claims, and export-excluded fields.
- Named native modernization queries and representative scale snapshots for the storage decision; these do not require Neo4j.

Use committed license-clean representative fixtures. The CardDemo corpus may supply source categories, but labels and reduced fixtures must be reviewed for redistribution, deterministic expected evidence, and coverage of parser limitations such as standalone copybooks.

**Labeling:**
Two independent qualified reviewers label assertion support, semantic correctness, missing context, and expected disposition using stable case/claim IDs. A third reviewer adjudicates disagreements. Security and graph-equality labels are code-derived and independently reviewed. Keep at least 20% as a hidden holdout until the release candidate. An LLM judge may prioritize review only after >= 0.70 correlation with adjudicated human scores on a separate calibration split; its score cannot waive a deterministic or human gate.

### Storage Projection Decision Gate

Neo4j remains outside the AI framework and harness. Phase 9 records native p50/p95 latency, memory, graph size, refresh cost, result correctness, and Cypher snapshot fidelity for approved named modernization queries on representative estate data. The final ADR must default to defer unless at least one native query misses a pre-agreed target or required expressiveness, and every gate in `STORAGE-ADR.md` is evidenced. Even a passed gate authorizes only a separately planned rebuildable projection; SQLite remains authoritative and v1.0 gains no Neo4j availability, sync, dual-write, or reverse-write dependency.

## 6. Guardrails

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Provider authorization | Missing/ambiguous policy or disallowed provider, model, endpoint/region, scope, data class, or retention mode | Block before context serialization; record metadata-only denial |
| Scope and ownership health | Missing scope, out-of-scope evidence, unknown IDs, or nonzero unowned/misprefixed records in selected release scope | Block dispatch or persistence |
| Context/redaction budget | Disallowed content, canary, secret class, or context budget violation | Block; no payload or raw error logging |
| Closed-schema and semantic validation | Malformed envelope, unknown field, invalid bounds, stale/out-of-scope citation, unsupported transition, or deterministic mutation request | Reject entire claim envelope; persist no claim |
| Deterministic write isolation | AI path attempts to mutate deterministic rows or close deterministic findings | Deny and page as a release/security defect |
| Audit health | Required metadata-only audit cannot be written under policy | Fail the AI request closed |

### Offline (Flywheel)

| Metric | Sampling Strategy | Action on Degradation |
|--------|------------------|-----------------------|
| Schema/citation/unsupported rates | Full reference set by version; all production validation failures | Block release or experimental promotion; inspect by failure family |
| Human outcome distribution | All experimental claims initially; later stratified by provider/model, construct, confidence band, and gap family | Recalibrate rubric/prompt/context; never promote on confidence alone |
| Reviewer disagreement | All dual-labeled reference claims and sampled production claims | Adjudicate, clarify labels, and expand counterexamples |
| Recurring gap families | Rank all reviewed gaps by frequency, affected programs, and inbound references | Create deterministic parser/extractor backlog candidates after expert review |
| Quality/cost/latency drift | Compare versioned cohorts and weight retries, denials, long outputs, and provider changes | Freeze rollout and investigate signal-metric divergence |
| Canary sink scans | Every release run and every telemetry/export configuration change | Immediate release block and incident handling on any forbidden occurrence |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** Native metadata-only structured telemetry. Export through the project's approved telemetry path if configured; no raw prompt, source context, response, rationale, or source path is included by default. Arize Phoenix is deferred pending explicit security/retention approval.

**Key Metrics to Track:**
- Deterministic extraction failures and `PARSE_UNRESOLVED`/`EXTERNAL_UNRESOLVED` counts by normalized reason, parser/extractor version, and authorized scope identifiers.
- AI requests by allowed/denied policy outcome, provider/model, schema validation result, citation validation result, provider failure class, retries, tokens, latency, and estimated cost.
- Claims by lifecycle state and review outcome: inferred, confirmed, contradicted, rejected, stale, superseded, and pending.
- Unsupported-claim rate and citation-entailment sample score by corpus/label version, provider/model, construct family, and confidence band.
- AI-on/off deterministic snapshot comparison result and ownership-health result for release canaries.
- Recurring reviewed gap families ranked by count, affected programs, inbound references, and trend.
- Cypher snapshot export success, scope/filter manifest digest, deterministic rebuild equality, age, and canary scan result; never label this synchronization lag.

**Alert Thresholds:**
- Page/block immediately on any canary leak, unauthorized outbound byte, deterministic snapshot mismatch, AI deterministic-row mutation attempt, out-of-scope persisted citation, or owner/misprefix acceptance failure.
- Block release if persisted-claim schema/citation validity is below 100%, unsupported exposed claims exceed 0%, required construct-family threshold fails, or lifecycle transitions differ from expected outcomes.
- Alert on any production validation failure-rate regression above the approved baseline, provider failure spike, unresolved-count discontinuity after parser/extractor change, or required audit-health failure.
- Warn and investigate cost/latency or review-distribution drift using baselines established by the first accepted provider cohort; do not invent static operational thresholds before that baseline exists.

**Smart Sampling Strategy:**
Initially review 100% of experimental claims. After promotion criteria are met, retain all policy denials, validator failures, contradictions, rejections, stale/superseded transitions, low-evidence/high-confidence claims, retries, unusual token/latency cases, prompt-injection detections, and changed provider/prompt/context/schema cohorts. Add stratified random samples across construct and gap families so frequent easy cases do not crowd out rare IDMS/CICS/SQL, parse-gap, missing-artifact, or cross-repository cases. Compare user/reviewer signals with metric scores and investigate divergence.

**AI exposure status:** Experimental only until every critical gate passes on the frozen reference set and security canaries.

**Neo4j status:** Deferred decision gate. It is neither selected framework nor Phase 9 implementation. The expected v1.0 outcome remains SQLite authority plus a scoped one-way rebuildable Cypher snapshot unless measured evidence supports a separately planned projection.

**Unresolved before execution planning:** Approved providers/deployments, organization-specific retention and region policy, named benchmark queries and numeric latency targets, representative estate scale, production telemetry sink approval, and final corpus redistribution/label ownership.
