The algorithm is RESEARCH Q6, made precise:

The base symbol is `container/name`, or `name` when the stack is empty. Keep a per-base-symbol occurrence counter: the first occurrence uses the base symbol, the k-th occurrence for k of 2 or more appends `#k`. Push the entry.
