## WR-04 decision

Review WR-04: `opensWithDataDescription` in the fork picks the data header for any member whose first code token is `EXEC` or a level number, and the procedure header otherwise. Probing the pinned producer on invented members confirmed what that does. A member opening with `EXEC CICS …` is wrapped as data and grades red with no member observation. FD/SD members are wrapped as procedure and grade red with no member observation. A member opening with `COPY X.` followed by data is wrapped as procedure, grades red, and keeps only its COPY observation. A `:TAG:` pseudo-text member grades red with no member observation.

Gortex cannot fix the classification without a producer change. `AnalyzeCopybook` takes no variant, and wrapping bytes on the Gortex side would invent coordinates (research Option C, rejected). A fork change would need its own user-confirmed push plan, which this gap closure does not plan. Gortex mitigates it by making the outcome visible:

- `cobol_copybook_wrapper` (data or procedure) is a fact read from the wrapper program's division observation.
- `prov_analysis_absence=copybook_content_unmapped` is set when a degraded analysis mapped nothing.
- The docs row names the window.

The partial case remains. A degraded member that still mapped something, such as a leading COPY's imports edge, shows its loss only through its grade, its reasons, and the wrapper value. Phase 5 parse findings (`PARSE_UNRESOLVED`) are the place to localize it.
